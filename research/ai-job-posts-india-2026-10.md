## What it says
- **A is closed for now.** All 11 mid-level AI Engineer posts require hands-on LLM work (about 7 say
  "production" outright). Python is mandatory in 10 of 11. Only the junior roles (₹7-10L) accept projects.
- **B is the door.** 6 of 11 accept no prior production AI. The best fits want deep React/TS, streaming
  UIs and performance, and treat AI as a bonus: Avoca AI, Sarvam frontend (x2), Auxo AI.
- **D has shifted.** 3 of 5 plain full-stack posts now mention AI (mostly AI coding tools).
- **C is a trap right now.** Real engineering FDE roles require production AI. The rest are sales or
  customer success with an FDE label.
- **What moves you up a bucket:** one LLM feature shipped and running on a live product.
- **Degree:** almost nothing gates on CS. Mechanical engineering satisfies "Engineering or related".
- **Interviews (stated in 8 of 37):** startups lean on take-homes and founder calls. DSA + system design
  was stated only once (bucket D).
- **Pay:** AI-titled roles don't clearly pay more than good full-stack roles at funded startups.
