## What "full deep-drill" means (Dwellworks + CloudForestX)

Every bullet gets the red treatment **regardless of its defend-map color**. Claude teaches the *why* and
*how* behind it (grounded in the repo + `../project-knowledge-base/`), then Tarun explains it back cold and
fields the follow-up ladder. 🟢 here means "you built it," not "skip it" — the goal is that you can walk an
interviewer through **any line** of these two projects without notes. D20 and the D10/D11/D21 set may each
run over more than one sitting; that's expected.

## Suggested sequence

T0 → T1 (Dwellworks, all of it) → T2 (CloudForestX, all of it) → T3 → T4 → T5 → T6 → T7 → T8.

Slot 🔴 re-drills back in whenever `progress.md` says they're due. **Don't advance while a Dwellworks or
CloudForestX sub-topic is below the gate** — those two are the spine of the interview.

## Session unit

One drill per sitting: teach + closed-book quiz + grade + open floor. ~15-25 min. Say **"drill me"** and
Claude picks up the next open drill.
