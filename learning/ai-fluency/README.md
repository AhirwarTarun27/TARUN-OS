## Why this exists, and why it is boxed

`mission/plan.md:106` cut AI fluency on 2026-08-09: *"a story you tell, not a subject you study."*
That call was right about the topic and wrong about one thing — on 2026-08-11 he published
[`ai-dev-workflow`](https://github.com/AhirwarTarun27/ai-dev-workflow), the extraction of roughly a year
of working this way across his own repos and his work project. **He now owns a public artifact he
intends to screen-share.** The practice is real and long-running; what is missing is the *vocabulary* to
narrate it and the *rehearsal* to do it under pressure. That is not a study track, that is an undefended
surface, and it is exactly what `cv-defense` exists for.

> **Calibration, and it governs every file here.** He is not new to this. The teaching names things he
> already does and supplies the words for them; it does not introduce him to AI-assisted development.
> **Where a lesson explains a concept he already practises, the rep is the sentence, not the idea.**

So the cut is reversed inside a box:

| The box | Why |
|---|---|
| **7 sittings. Ends 2026-08-22.** | 14 weeks and 8 tracks is the arithmetic that produced nothing in July |
| **1 hour, office bench time (4:00-6:30pm)** | `daily/schedule.md:36` — that block is adherence-only. This costs nothing that scores |
| **Unscored. Not in `state.json`. Not on the floor.** | `mission/plan.md:117` is untouched: 1 CV drill + 1 coding rep + 3 applications |
| **Every sitting ends in a spoken rep** | `daily/schedule.md:91` — *"Reading for a rep you never perform is not a rep"* |
| **D32 is the exam, not a new gate** | `cv-defense/roadmap.md` row 12 already exists. No second scoreboard |

## The read budget

A sitting reads **`survival-sheet.md` + the one lesson for that day.** That is it.

- **NEVER glob `lessons/`.** Six lessons is six lessons; loading all of them is six times the cost for
  no gain.
- **NEVER glob `learning-records/`.** They exist to be re-read at D32 and never again.
- Depth about his own repo lives in `../cv-defense/answers/devflow/`, **referenced, never copied**
  (`cv-defense/roadmap.md:81` — duplicated teaching drifts apart).

## The rules

1. **Never claim a capability he has not exercised.** The whole point of the honest-boundary sections
   is that a crisp *"I've never trained a model"* reads as senior and a mumbled *"I've touched a bit of
   that"* is fatal.
2. **The office project is never named, described, or characterised** — not here, not in a lesson, not
   in an answer file, not on the CV. "My current project" is the whole sentence.
3. **Tier 3 stays skipped.** Transformer internals, LoRA, vector index internals, MLOps. Not his job,
   and studying them is how the box breaks.
4. **A term he cannot say out loud is a term he does not have.** The lesson is not the rep; the commute
   is the rep.
