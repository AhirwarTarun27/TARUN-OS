## How a rule gets written

Only `/reading` writes here, and only at the end of a drill. The bar:

1. **One rule per drill. Never two.** Two rules means neither gets run.
2. **In your words, not the author's.** A rule you can't phrase yourself, you don't hold.
3. **Runnable in 14 days.** "Be more patient with money" is not a rule — it's a mood.
   "Before any buy over ₹5K, wait 48h and write down what I expect to feel after" is a rule.
4. **It must be falsifiable.** If nothing could happen in 14 days that would kill it, it's not a
   rule, it's a slogan — and it doesn't get written.
5. **No honest rule? Write nothing.** A drill that ends with "this chapter was true but it doesn't
   change anything I do" is a **successful** drill. Manufacturing a rule to have an output is the
   single worst failure mode of this whole track.
