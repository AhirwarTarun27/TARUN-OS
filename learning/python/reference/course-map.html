The path: 17 Exercism concepts + 8 bridges

Exercism is your one resource. Each chapter gets a lesson here, written for a JS developer. Bridges cover what the AI path needs and Exercism doesn't teach.

Are you on the right path? Checked 2026-10-03 Yes, for the core language. The 17 concepts come straight from the track's own config file. They cover every data type, loops, classes and generators, which is the Python you'll read in any AI course.

What Exercism leaves out, and the AI path needs on day one: f-strings (Exercism's exercise for it is unfinished), keyword arguments, comprehensions, try/except, imports and pip, JSON, type hints and async. Those are the bridge lessons below. They're short, they live here, and you don't need a second website for them.
