# Starting floor: working dev, gap is interview *framing* not concepts

Established at kickoff (2026-07-08). Tarun is a working full-stack developer who ships real products
(JsonBeam, GradeJar) on Cloudflare Workers with zero-backend architectures. He has genuine hands-on
building intuition — this is not a beginner learning "what is a server."

**Implication for ZPD:** the gap to close is *interview framing* — the vocabulary (vertical/horizontal,
replication, sharding, stateless), the tradeoff articulation, and above all the ability to **drive a design
out loud** with a framework. Do NOT spend lesson time re-teaching basic web mechanics he uses daily. Teach at
the altitude of "how do I present and defend this in 45 minutes." Every concept gets a "say this in the
interview" one-liner; every lesson ends with a design-out-loud drill.

**Mission grounding:** active job switch, frontend-heavy full-stack, 2-3 month target (from 2026-07-07).
Prep is mainly design-out-loud, not heavy implementation. See [[MISSION.md]].

**Not yet demonstrated:** no evidence yet of him *narrating* the scaling ladder or running the 4-step
framework unaided. Lesson 1 covered the ladder (Ch 1); the win is banked only once he narrates it cold in the
design-out-loud drill. Watch for that in the next session before advancing depth.
