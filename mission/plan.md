## The two mechanisms that keep this alive

**The minimum viable day (the floor).** 1 CV drill + 1 machine-coding-or-interview-qa rep +
3 applications. Roughly 3 hours.

> Your failure mode is binary: 6/7 or 1/7, and the collapses are **anxiety-triggered, not
> discipline-triggered** (07-15 says so outright). A plan that assumes calm days will fail, because
> you won't have many. The floor exists so a bad day is a *floor day*, not a zero — which is what
> currently turns one bad day into nine dark ones.

**No mode switch on interview days.** The board keeps running; one hour swaps in for the JD. Nothing
enters that window you haven't already touched once. See `mission/interview-sprint.md`.

> Your conversion improves with the **number of interviews**, not the days before each one. The
> fourth goes better because of the three before it, not because you crammed.
