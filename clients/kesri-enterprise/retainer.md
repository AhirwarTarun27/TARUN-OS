## Be honest about what this is

**₹3,000/year is ₹250/month.** It is not a retainer; it is a gesture. That is fine — it was a conscious choice for a relative and a case study — but **do not let it become the mental anchor for what a retainer is worth.**

The real rate card:
- **Care** — ₹1.5K-4K/**month**: hosting, domain renewal, uptime, backups, small edits
- **Growth** — ₹5K-15K/**month**: Care + actively keeping them found (new pages, GBP posts, review chasing, AI-search work, and the monthly report)

**Client #2 pays market.** See `references/client-delivery-playbook.md`.

The value of the Kesri retainer is not the ₹3K. It is that it keeps Tarun in the account, producing a monthly report — which is what generates the referrals.
