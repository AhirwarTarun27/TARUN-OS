## Account ownership — every one in the CLIENT's name

Tarun is the **manager**, never the owner. Holding a client's domain is not leverage — it is a reputation, and in a district this size the referral engine runs in reverse just as fast as it runs forward.

| Account | Owner | Verified | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Google Business Profile | **CLIENT** | [ ] | Their Google account owns it; Tarun = manager |
| Google Search Console | **CLIENT** | [ ] | |
| Business email | **CLIENT** | [ ] | |
| Hosting (Cloudflare) | Tarun (shared) | [ ] | **Disclosed to client** — see below |
| Directory listings | **CLIENT** | [ ] | Created with their email |

**The hosting exception, stated honestly:** the site runs on Tarun's shared Cloudflare account — that's what makes the free tier work. **Say it out loud in the handover doc.** The code is theirs, the domain is theirs, and it can be moved if they ever leave. *A dependency the client knows about is a service. One they discover later is a trap.*

## Show the win — the whole phase

**Not an emailed link.** In person or screen-shared.

- [ ] **Search Console** — *"These are the actual words people typed to find you."* ← the moment
- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — the pin, the calls, the direction requests
- [ ] **The site on THEIR phone**, loading in under a second
- [ ] **A live test enquiry** — Tarun submits the form and their phone buzzes in front of them. **The single best moment in the engagement.**
- [ ] **The AI prompt-check baseline** — honestly, as a baseline, with the plan

A client who doesn't *feel* the value doesn't refer and doesn't renew. They see a website and assume that's simply what they paid for. The value is invisible unless you show it.

## Collect — all four, in one conversation, while the win is on screen

- [ ] **Final payment**
- [ ] **Testimonial** — specific, not "great work". *"You had a brochure; now buyers find you on Google."*
- [ ] **Written portfolio permission** — name, logo, screenshots, results. **Written.** A verbal yes from a relative is not permission to publish.
- [ ] **Referral ask — direct.** Ask for **ONE** introduction, over WhatsApp, with the message pre-drafted.

  > *"Who else do you know who needs this? Any supplier or plant you work with who has no website, or has one nobody can find?"*

  "Let me know if you hear of anyone" produces exactly nothing.
