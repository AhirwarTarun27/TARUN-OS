## The monthly report — the deliverable

**The report IS the retainer.** Not admin around the work — the thing they're buying. Skip it and you're charging for something invisible, and invisible things get cancelled.

Send it **every month, even in a quiet month.** Honest bad news builds more trust than silence, and it's what makes the good months believable.
