## Adjustments carried into August

Already made, on 08-09, and they came straight out of this month's evidence:

1. **8 tracks → 4.** `sysdesign`, `reading`, `workout`, `project`, `ai-fluency` cut; `dsa` parked
   to Phase B. 14 weeks × 8 tracks is arithmetic that produces nothing, and July proved it.
2. **A floor:** 1 CV drill + 1 coding rep + 3 applications. Hitting it scores the day a WIN
   regardless of block count — because July is binary (6/7 or 1/7, almost nothing between) and a
   plan that assumes calm days will fail.
3. **Two kinds of time.** The two things that die on interruption (the timed coding rep, the
   drill) moved into 9pm–12am, the only hours nobody can take. `interview-qa`'s placement
   diagnosis from 07-16 generalized to the whole schedule.
4. **A countdown.** `/console` is the front door; the board re-cuts targets so it can never
   accuse.

**The one adjustment July's evidence demands and August has not yet delivered: log every day.**
Both dark stretches produced nothing, and the month-end compression this file replaces was itself
11 days late.
