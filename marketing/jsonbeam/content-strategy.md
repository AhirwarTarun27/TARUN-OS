# JsonBeam — Content Strategy & Calendar

Written 2026-07-03. Everything here is draftable by the AIOS; Tarun approves and posts
(inside the shared weekly Sunday session with GradeJar — cadences below are weekly-batch
targets under the weekly-first engine, 2026-07-10, not daily obligations).
Voice: dev-to-dev, plain, zero hype. Always disclose "I built this" in communities. Never
claim privacy benefits before the claim is verified end-to-end.

## The rhythm (no school year here)

Dev traffic has no season, but it has a pulse:

| Pattern | What it means |
|---|---|
| Weekday-heavy (Mon-Thu peak) | Publish and pitch Tue-Thu, US morning. Weekends are dead: batch prep then. |
| Late-Dec + big-holiday dips | Don't launch or pitch Dec 15 - Jan 5. |
| Launch windows are self-made | HN -> newsletters -> Product Hunt replace seasonality. Stack them inside ~2 weeks so each feeds the next. |
| Error searches are constant | Parse-error pages compound year-round. Ship them early. |

## Evergreen content (compounds forever)

- **Site pages (SEO+GEO):** the 17 tool pages (front-loaded answer + FAQ schema), error-message pages ("Unexpected token < in JSON at position 0: what it means and the fix"), converter guides, comparisons ("JsonBeam vs JSONLint", "why not just use my IDE").
- **Dev.to/Hashnode syndication:** site guides republished with canonical tags. Feeds the GEO consensus signal.
- **GitHub presence:** awesome-list entries live forever and keep referring.

## Viral-potential plays (swing occasionally, never depend on)

- **The privacy audit (flagship):** "I checked what 10 online JSON formatters actually do with your JSON", with network-tab receipts. Publish on site, syndicate to dev.to, seed on HN/Reddit. Positions the privacy story with proof instead of claims. Everything else references this piece.
- **Show HN:** one shot, Days 31-60. See the action plan.
- **Visualizer screenshots on X/Reddit:** pretty JSON graph images travel (JSON Crack grew on exactly this). "Here's a famous API response, visualized."
- **JSON horror stories (engagement ammo):** trailing commas in prod, BOM bytes, 2GB single-line files. Relatable dev pain earns comment karma, which earns account credibility.

## Repurposing chain (one idea, six assets)

One JSON tip or error ->
1. FAQ block or error page on jsonbeam.com (SEO/GEO)
2. Dev.to article section (syndicated, canonical)
3. Reddit comment ammunition (when the error shows up in a thread)
4. X post (with a visual when possible)
5. HN comment-thread ammo
6. (later) 60-second screencast script

The AIOS maintains `marketing/jsonbeam/idea-bank.md` (created by /marketing) and runs
every new idea through this chain so nothing is written once and used once.
