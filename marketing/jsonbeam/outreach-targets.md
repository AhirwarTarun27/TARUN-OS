# JsonBeam — Outreach Targets

Maintained by `/marketing`. Status: `todo` -> `pitched YYYY-MM-DD` -> `followed-up` -> `won / passed`.
Pitch from personal email only. One follow-up at day 7-10, then stop.
Hook for all of them: fast, free, all-in-one JSON toolkit (17 tools) that never uploads
your data, built by a solo dev.
**Timing rule:** nothing gets pitched until the on-page polish + privacy verification are
done. One first impression.

## Tier C — directories (one-time batch, AIOS pre-fills copy)

AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, LibHunt, StackShare, BetaList, free-tool directories.
Skip anything demanding payment. Skip AI-tool directories (JsonBeam is not an AI tool).
Product Hunt is a launch (Days 31-60 decision), not a directory listing.
