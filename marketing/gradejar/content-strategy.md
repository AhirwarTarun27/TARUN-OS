## The seasonal spine (US school year)

| Window | What's happening | Content mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Jul (now)** | Teachers off, planners planning. Editors build back-to-school lists. Pinterest back-to-school searches ramp NOW (pinners plan 2-3 months out). | Foundation: pages, pins, pitches. Pitch every roundup THIS MONTH. |
| **Aug 1 - Sep 15** | Back-to-school. Gradebook setup, grading-scale decisions, EZ-grader demand returns. THE teacher acquisition window. | Max output: gradebook setup content everywhere. |
| **Sep - Nov** | Routine grading. First progress reports (~6 wks in). | Sustain: workflow tips, backlinks, YouTube how-tos. |
| **Dec + May** | Finals. "Final grade calculator" demand up ~100x (RogerHub effect). Student-heavy. | Student-side content: final-grade pages, Reddit student subs. |
| **Jan** | New semester, fresh gradebooks, resolutions. | Mini back-to-school replay. |
| **Mar-Apr** | Testing season, GPA anxiety, college admissions. | GPA-tool content. |

## Evergreen content (compounds forever)

- **Site pages (SEO+GEO):** the pSEO sets (`/ez-grader/N-questions`, need-on-final variants, grading scales), plus explainers: "How teachers average grades", "Weighted vs unweighted", "What is a 10-point grading scale". Every page: answer in first sentence, FAQ schema, tool above fold.
- **Pinterest:** grading-scale cheat sheets, "grade faster" tip pins, gradebook-setup pins. Pins rank for years.
- **YouTube (when activated):** "Set up a private gradebook in 5 min", "Grade 40 tests in one tap", "EZ grader online walkthrough". Evergreen, AI-cited, feeds Shorts.
- **Quora one-time batch:** top 20 evergreen grading/GPA questions, answered properly with disclosure.

## Viral-potential plays (swing occasionally, never depend on)

- Reddit r/Teachers value post in late Aug: "I built a free gradebook that never uploads student data - no logins, works offline. Tear it apart." Honest maker post + privacy hook + good timing = front-page potential.
- The FERPA/privacy angle pitched as a story (SOS/Qwoted/edtech press): "Why your gradebook shouldn't know your students' names."
- A genuinely useful printable (grading-scale mega cheat sheet PDF) seeded on Pinterest + shared free in FB groups: earns saves, links, and email signups.
- Dec finals week: "what do I need on my final" interactive meme-adjacent content for student subs. RogerHub owns this: we take the overflow.

## Repurposing chain (one idea, seven assets)

One grading tip ->
1. FAQ block on the relevant gradejar.com page (SEO/GEO)
2. Pinterest pin (tip as cheat-sheet graphic)
3. Reddit comment ammunition (when the question appears)
4. Newsletter snippet
5. Bluesky/X post
6. Quora answer paragraph
7. (later) 60-second YouTube Short script

The AIOS maintains an idea bank in `marketing/gradejar/idea-bank.md` (created by the /marketing skill) and runs every new idea through this chain so nothing is written once and used once.

## Community engagement rules

- 90/10: nine helps per one mention. Answer fully in-thread; the link is a bonus, not the answer.
- Always disclose maker status. Teachers respect makers, they torch marketers.
- Never post the same text twice anywhere.
- Every external post in Tarun's voice gets his eyes before it ships (hard rule from CLAUDE.md).
- Never automate posting to Reddit/FB. Drafts only.
