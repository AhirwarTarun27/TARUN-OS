  salary. Decided 2026-08-09 while calm, precisely so the November version of him doesn't have to.

### What changed on 2026-08-09, and why

The 07-07 list treated the job hunt as one of three parallel priorities and ranked **DSA as the
gate**. Two things broke it:

1. **It contradicted his own 07-29 triage**, which had already demoted DSA out of the top 3. Two
   contradictory boards ran side by side for three weeks and he paid the tax of trying to obey both.
2. **Eight tracks in 14 weeks is arithmetic that produces nothing** — and that is empirically what
   happened. In the 26 days to 2026-08-09: zero machine-coding reps ever, 1 of 14 CV drills started,
   2 DSA problems both `watched` with rungs 21 days overdue, and the daily log dark for 11 days.

**Cut to zero:** system design / Xu Vol 1 · AI fluency as a study track · `reading` · `workout` ·
`project` · Astro as a track · .NET as prep. **Parked:** DSA until 22 Sept, then capped.

Nothing here was cut for being unimportant. It was cut because the mission has a date on it.
**Do not restore a cut track without a real interview punishing him for it** — that is what the
sacrificial wave and the Phase 2 gap queue exist to detect.
