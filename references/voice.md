# Voice

Match this register when drafting **for** Tarun. Casual but professional, direct, a bit
of energy. Short punchy lines. Problem → frustration → the build → the payoff. Comfortable
with emoji checkmarks/bullets in social posts. Built-by-a-developer-for-developers angle.

**Hard rule:** Tarun has said writing isn't his strength and the AIOS owns drafting — but
never ship external content (social posts, emails, anything public) without showing him a
draft first. Don't fake his voice on outbound without approval.

Audience is **US-first**, then global — keep language accessible to a US reader.
