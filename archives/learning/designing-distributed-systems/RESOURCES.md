# Designing Distributed Systems Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Designing Distributed Systems_, 2nd ed. — Brendan Burns (O'Reilly, 2024)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-distributed-systems/9781098156343/)
  THE primary text for this track. 16 chapters, 5 parts. Use for: the canonical framing of every pattern, in the author's own words. Read the chapter *before* or *after* each lesson.
- [Free eBook: _Designing Distributed Systems_ (1st ed. PDF) — Microsoft](https://info.microsoft.com/rs/157-GQE-382/images/EN-CNTNT-eBook-DesigningDistributedSystems.pdf)
  Full 1st-edition PDF, free and legal. Missing the 2nd-ed chapters (AI inference, failure patterns, expanded concepts) but great for the core patterns. Use for: reading the source when you don't have O'Reilly access.
- [Chapter 1: Introduction (2nd ed.)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-distributed-systems/9781098156343/ch01.html)
  Use for: why patterns/practices/components matter — the "shared language" argument.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/ExperiencedDevs](https://reddit.com/r/ExperiencedDevs) and [r/cscareerquestions](https://reddit.com/r/cscareerquestions)
  Use for: real system-design-interview debriefs and what patterns actually get asked.
- [Hello Interview — System Design](https://www.hellointerview.com/learn/system-design)
  High-signal, interview-specific. Use for: pressure-testing whether you can *apply* a pattern under interview conditions, not just define it.
